Monolith vs Microservices

Monolith
Microservices
Deployment
Single unit shipped and deployed together
Independent services deployed on their own schedule
Scaling
Whole app scales together, even unused parts
Each service scales independently on demand
Team Coordination
Shared codebase requires tight coordination
Teams own separate services and move independently
Fault Isolation
One failure can bring down the entire system
A single service failure stays contained
VerdictFor teams over 20 engineers, microservices are the recommended architecture.
