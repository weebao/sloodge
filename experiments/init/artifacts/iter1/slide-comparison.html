Monolith vs Microservices

Monolith
Microservices
Single codebase — simple to build and deploy early on
Independent services — each deployed and scaled on its own
Scaling means scaling the whole app, even one hot path
Scale only the services under load, saving infra cost
One team's change can block or break another's release
Teams ship independently with clear service boundaries
Easy to reason about, but growth breeds tangled dependencies
Higher operational complexity — needs strong DevOps maturity
Verdict For engineering orgs with over 20 engineers, we recommend microservices — team-level autonomy and independent scaling outweigh the added operational overhead.
